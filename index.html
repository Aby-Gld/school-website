Every Child Deserves a Chance

Antarbharti Ashram is a non-profit childcare and school in Nagpur, India, providing education, care, nutritious meals, and support to children from low-income families.

With the right care and opportunity, every child can build a brighter future.

Our Mission

To create a safe, nurturing environment where children feel valued, supported, and inspired to learn.

Our Vision

We believe every child deserves a chance to learn, grow, and thrive. Antarbharti Ashram provides safe spaces and quality education for children who need it most.
